# Your First Canvas

This guide walks you through creating your first canvas in Eldoria.

## Creating a Canvas

1. **Open Strategic Workspace**
   - Click the **Layout** icon in the sidebar (top-left)
   - Or navigate to the root URL

2. **Create New Canvas**
   - Click **+ New Canvas** button in the middle panel
   - Your new canvas appears with a text editor ready

3. **Start Writing**
   - Click in the editor area
   - Type your content using Markdown

## Adding Content Parts

Canvases support multiple types of content:

### Text
Just start typing. Markdown formatting is supported.

### Images
1. Click the **🖼️ Image** button in the toolbar
2. Upload from your device

### Mathematical Formulas
1. Click the **∫ Formula** button
2. Enter LaTeX syntax (e.g., `E = mc^2`)
3. See live preview as you type

## Saving Your Work

Eldoria auto-saves your canvas to the cloud. Watch the status bar for:
- **"Saving..."** — Changes being synced
- **"All changes saved"** — Work is safe
