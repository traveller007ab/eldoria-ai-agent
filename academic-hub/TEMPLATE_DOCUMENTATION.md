# Thesis Template Documentation

This document provides guidelines for structuring your thesis according to Rivers State University (RSU) standards.

## Standard Thesis Structure

### Title Page
```
[UNIVERSITY NAME]
[DEPARTMENT NAME]

[THESIS TITLE]
(Specific, concise, maximum 20 words)

by

[YOUR FULL NAME]
[MATRICULATION NUMBER]

A Thesis Submitted in Partial Fulfillment of the Requirements
for the Award of [DEGREE NAME]
[Rivers State University]

[YEAR]
```

### Abstract
- 150-300 words
- Single paragraph
- Include: purpose, methodology, key findings, conclusions
- Keywords: 3-5 terms

### Certification Page
```
This is to certify that this thesis was prepared by [YOUR NAME]
with matriculation number [MATRIC NO.]
under my supervision and has been submitted for examination.

________________________
[Supervisor Name]
[Title/Position]
[Date]
```

### Dedication (Optional)
- Brief dedication statement
- Usually 1-2 lines

### Acknowledgments
- Express gratitude to supervisors, family, colleagues
- 1 page maximum

### Table of Contents
- Automatic generation recommended
- Include all chapters and major sections
- Page numbers must be accurate

### List of Tables
- Numbered sequentially
- Include titles and page numbers

### List of Figures
- Numbered sequentially
- Include titles and page numbers

## Formatting Guidelines

### Margins
- Top: 1 inch (2.54 cm)
- Bottom: 1 inch (2.54 cm)
- Left: 1.5 inches (3.81 cm)
- Right: 1 inch (2.54 cm)

### Font
- Body Text: Times New Roman, 12pt
- Headings: 14pt (chapter), 12pt (subheadings)
- Consistent throughout

### Line Spacing
- Body text: Double spacing (2.0)
- Block quotes: Single spacing
- Footnotes: Single spacing
- References: Double spacing

### Page Numbering
- Preliminary pages: Roman numerals (i, ii, iii...)
- Main content: Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...)
- Position: Bottom center

### Heading Format
**Chapter Title:** Centered, 14pt, Bold, ALL CAPS

**1.1 Section Title:** Left-aligned, 12pt, Bold

**1.1.1 Subsection:** Left-aligned, 12pt, Regular

### Citations

**In-Text Citations:**
- Single author: (Author, Year)
- Two authors: (Author1 & Author2, Year)
- Three or more: (Author et al., Year)
- Multiple sources: (Author1, Year; Author2, Year)

**Direct Quotes:**
- Short (< 40 words): Include page number
- Long (40+ words): Block quote, no quotation marks

### Tables and Figures

**Tables:**
- Numbered sequentially (Table 1, Table 2...)
- Title above table
- Source below table

**Figures:**
- Numbered sequentially (Figure 1, Figure 2...)
- Title below figure
- Source below figure

## Reference List Format (APA 7th Edition)

### Journal Article
Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxxx

### Book
Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book* (Edition). Publisher.

### Chapter in Edited Book
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In Editor B. B. (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. xx-xx). Publisher.

### Website
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of page. Site Name. https://www.url

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Inconsistent formatting**: Stick to one style throughout
2. **Missing citations**: Every claim needs a source
3. **Incorrect references**: Verify APA 7th Edition format
4. **Poor grammar**: Use grammar checking tools
5. **Inadequate literature review**: Include recent sources (last 5 years)
6. **Weak methodology**: Justify your research design
7. **Missing page numbers**: Check all pages
8. **Incorrect margins**: Measure your margins
